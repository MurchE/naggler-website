Security

Report a vulnerability.

Found a way Naggler could expose someone's data or be made to do something it shouldn't? Please tell us.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

How to report

Email feedback@naggler.com with "Security" in the subject. Include the Naggler version and platform (macOS or Windows), what you found and how to reproduce it.

What you can expect from us. We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days, keep you informed while we investigate, and credit you if you would like. We do not promise a fix date.

What we ask of you

  • Give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.
  • Test only with your own copy of the app and your own accounts.
  • If you encounter anyone else's personal data, stop, do not keep it, and tell us. Leave it out of your report.
  • Do not disrupt our services (no denial-of-service or load testing), and do not use social engineering or phishing.
  • Do not engage in extortion.

Safe harbor

We will consider your security research to be authorized if you make a good faith effort to comply with this policy during your security research. If your activities violate certain restrictions in our Terms, we will waive those restrictions for the limited purpose of allowing security research. We will not sue you for attempting to circumvent the technological safeguards we have put in place to protect the applications in scope. If a third party takes legal action against you for activities carried out in accordance with this policy, we will make this authorization known.

For inadvertent, good-faith violations of this policy, we will not take civil action or file a report with law enforcement. Before doing anything that may be inconsistent with or unaddressed by this policy, please contact us by submitting a report.

This safe harbor covers only legal claims under our control. It does not bind independent third parties, including the AI providers and the integration services (such as Todoist, Linear, Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do and Apple Reminders) that you connect Naggler to.

The safe-harbor wording is the disclose.io Simple Safe Harbor (CC0-1.0). The only change is that the waiver names our Terms rather than an "Acceptable Use Policy". The acknowledgement window follows CISA's vulnerability disclosure policy template.

Scope

In scope: the Naggler desktop apps for macOS and Windows (including the bundled naggler-mcp server and the in-app updater), the naggler.com website, and the Naggler Cloud relay Worker.

Out of scope: the third-party AI providers and integration services themselves, and the hosting platforms the site and relay run on. The privacy page lists what each provider receives.
